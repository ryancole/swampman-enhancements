## Summary

Accepts quests and turns them in automatically. When a reward has to be chosen, the choice is left to you.

## Description

**Quest Accept** takes the clicks out of questing. Talk to an NPC and finished quests are handed in, then new ones are picked up, with no button pressing. When a quest offers a choice of rewards, the reward page stays open so you pick the item and turn it in yourself; a quest with a fixed reward, or a single offered item, is turned in on the spot.

Hold Shift while talking to an NPC to do it by hand for a moment: read the quest text, decline something, or hand in at your own pace. Quests that would flag you for PvP are always left for a deliberate click.

**Commands**

- `/qa` — show what's on
- `/qa on` / `/qa off` — the whole addon
- `/qa accept on|off` — picking up quests
- `/qa turnin on|off` — handing in quests

Built for WoW Forever (1.60). No libraries, no configuration required.
